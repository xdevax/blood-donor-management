Find a Blood Donor, Faster

When a patient needs blood urgently, families lose critical hours phoning around for a matching donor. This system keeps a searchable record of registered donors and open blood requests, so hospitals and families can find a contact by blood group and city in seconds instead of hours.

What this system does

Four core record-keeping functions.

Donor Registration

Volunteers record their name, age, blood group, phone number and city. Each donor sets their own availability status.

Search & Filter

Filter the donor directory by blood group, city and availability to shortlist the people worth calling first.

Blood Requests

Hospitals and families log a request with the patient name, units required, hospital, city and an urgency level.

Request Tracking

Every request moves through Open, Fulfilled or Cancelled, so no request is silently forgotten.
